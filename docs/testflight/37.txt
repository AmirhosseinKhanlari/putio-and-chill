Build 37

Fixes from a full review of the app.

Apple TV
• If the built-in player gives up on a video, Apple's player takes over with a picture again (it used to play sound over a black screen) and goes on from where it was.
• A sleep timer set to "end of episode" no longer starts the next episode anyway; it waits for you.
• The Next Episode countdown starts over when you come back to the app instead of jumping straight to the next episode.
• With the Up Next screen showing, swiping up no longer lands on the small video.

Watching
• Opening an episode you finished on another device for a few seconds no longer un-marks it or drops it from Continue Watching.
• Signing out during an "Updating…" no longer brings the old account's library back.
• A file deleted on put.io no longer reappears in Continue Watching at every launch.
• Lock screen and Control Center controls keep working after Next Episode, and after closing a trailer while a video is in Picture in Picture.
• Starting another video while one is in Picture in Picture closes the first one instead of playing both.
• AirPlay: if the TV goes black after a subtitle is turned on, the app now notices and goes back to the subtitle-free stream by itself (about 8 seconds).

iPhone, iPad and Mac
• Cancelling a download right after starting it no longer leaves a hidden download on your device.
• Title pages opened from a notification, widget or Downloads show their artwork, name and year.
• Deleting from put.io, cancelling a transfer, creating a folder and checking a key now tell you when they fail.
• The keyboard goes away when you leave Search; the colored glow no longer changes under other tabs.
• Tapping a widget when the app isn't running now plays the video.
• Mac: AirPlay subtitles work (the app wasn't allowed to serve them before).

put.io
• Folders with more than 1,000 files are shown in full.
• A transfer deleted on put.io's site is removed instead of being checked forever.

Please try: on Apple TV, let an episode run out with a sleep timer set to "end of episode"; on iPhone, tap Next Episode and then check the lock-screen controls; on the onn TV, turn a subtitle on over AirPlay and see whether the picture comes back on its own.
